# Data policy

This public repository contains source code, schemas, tests, and source metadata. It does not redistribute local engineering archives, downloaded executables, CAD files, or third-party manuals unless their licenses explicitly allow redistribution.

Local documents belong under `data/raw/` or another path configured through `RKE_LOCAL_X1_ROOT`; both locations are excluded from Git. A source record should retain the original URL, retrieval time, content hash, product version, and license information when known.

Contributors are responsible for confirming that a document may be processed and redistributed. Generated indexes and extracted text may inherit restrictions from their source material.
